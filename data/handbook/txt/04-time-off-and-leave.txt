4.1 PAID TIME OFF

Paid time off, referred to as PTO, covers holiday and personal days. It does
not cover sickness, which is dealt with in section 4.3.

Regular full-time employees accrue PTO according to length of service:

  Years 1 and 2       15 days per year
  Years 3 to 5        20 days per year
  Year 6 and beyond   25 days per year

PTO accrues monthly, on the last day of each month, at one twelfth of the
annual entitlement. Part-time employees accrue on a pro-rated basis.

The new rate applies from the first full month after the service anniversary.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 REQUESTING AND CARRYING OVER PTO

Request PTO in the time-off system. Requests of three days or fewer should be
made at least 10 business days in advance. Requests of more than three days
should be made at least 20 business days in advance.

Your manager responds within five business days. Where two people on the same
team request the same dates, the earlier request takes precedence.

A maximum of five unused PTO days may be carried into the next calendar year.
Carried days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited.

On leaving the company, accrued and unused PTO is paid out at the current base
rate.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 SICK LEAVE

Regular full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year,
available in full from the first day of employment rather than accrued.

Sick leave covers the employee's own illness and the care of an immediate
family member. Notify your manager before core hours on each day of absence.

A medical note is required for an absence of more than three consecutive
scheduled days.

Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out on leaving.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 COMPANY HOLIDAYS

Larkfield Systems observes 11 paid company holidays each year:

   1. New Year's Day
   2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day
   3. Memorial Day
   4. Juneteenth
   5. Independence Day
   6. Labor Day
   7. Thanksgiving Day
   8. The day after Thanksgiving
   9. Christmas Eve
  10. Christmas Day
  11. One floating holiday, chosen by the employee

Where a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed on the preceding
Friday; where it falls on a Sunday it is observed on the following Monday.

The floating holiday must be taken within the calendar year and does not carry
over.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 PARENTAL LEAVE

Employees who have completed 12 months of service are eligible for paid
parental leave on the birth or adoption of a child:

  Primary caregiver     12 weeks at full pay
  Secondary caregiver    4 weeks at full pay

Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement. It may be taken
in up to three separate blocks with manager agreement.

Give People Operations at least 30 days notice where the timing is known.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

Five paid days are available on the death of an immediate family member, which
means a spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or
grandchild, including the equivalent relations of a spouse or domestic partner.

Two paid days are available on the death of any other relative.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.7 UNPAID LEAVE

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted at the discretion of your Vice
President once PTO is exhausted. Benefits continue during approved unpaid
leave, with the employee responsible for their share of premiums.
