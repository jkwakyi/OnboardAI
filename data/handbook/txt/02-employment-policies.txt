2.1 EMPLOYMENT CATEGORIES

Larkfield Systems uses three employment categories:

  Regular full-time   Scheduled for 40 hours per week. Eligible for the full
                      benefits package described in document 5.

  Regular part-time   Scheduled for at least 20 but fewer than 30 hours per
                      week. Eligible for paid time off on a pro-rated basis.
                      Not eligible for medical cover.

  Temporary           Engaged for a fixed period of no more than six months.
                      Not eligible for benefits.

Employees are further classified as exempt or non-exempt for overtime purposes.
Your offer letter states your category and classification.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 THE INTRODUCTORY PERIOD

New employees serve an introductory period of 90 calendar days from their start
date. During this period either party may end the employment relationship with
no notice period.

Completing the introductory period does not change the at-will nature of
employment. A formal review is held at the end of it, described in document 8.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 WORKING HOURS

The standard work week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday.

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Eastern Time. Employees are expected to be
contactable during core hours. Outside core hours, employees set their own
schedule in agreement with their manager.

Employees working more than six consecutive hours must take an unpaid meal
break of at least 30 minutes.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 WHERE YOU WORK

Three working arrangements are available:

  Office     Working from Baltimore or Portland on all scheduled days.
  Hybrid     Working from one of the two offices at least two days per week.
  Remote     Working from any location within the United States.

Your arrangement is agreed with your manager and recorded by People Operations.
Changing arrangement requires written manager approval and 30 days notice.

Employees who work remotely must have a workspace that allows confidential
calls. The equipment stipend in document 5 supports this.

Working from outside the United States requires written approval from both your
Vice President and the Legal team before travel.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 ATTENDANCE AND TIMEKEEPING

Notify your manager as early as possible, and no later than the start of core
hours, if you cannot work a scheduled day.

Non-exempt employees record hours worked in the timekeeping system by 17:00
Eastern Time on the last day of each pay period. Late submission delays
payment to the following pay date.

Three consecutive scheduled days of absence without notice is treated as
resignation.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6 ENDING EMPLOYMENT

Employees resigning are asked to give at least two weeks written notice to
their manager. Employees in roles at Director level or above are asked to give
four weeks.

An exit conversation with People Operations is offered to everyone leaving.
Company equipment must be returned within five business days of the last
working day, using the prepaid shipping label People Operations provides.

Final pay is issued on the next scheduled pay date after the last working day.
