8.1 BEFORE YOUR FIRST DAY

People Operations sends your equipment to arrive at least three business days
before your start date, along with instructions for setting up your accounts.

Contact people@larkfieldsystems.example if your equipment has not arrived two
business days before you start.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.2 YOUR FIRST WEEK

Day one runs to a fixed schedule:

  09:30  Welcome session with People Operations
  11:00  Account setup and security training enrolment
  13:00  Lunch with your team, in person or by video
  14:30  First meeting with your manager
  16:00  Introduction to your onboarding buddy

Within the first week you will also complete the security awareness training
described in document 7 and enrol in benefits as described in document 5.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.3 YOUR ONBOARDING BUDDY

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy, an experienced colleague
outside their direct reporting line.

The buddy answers day-to-day questions and meets the new employee weekly for
the first 90 days. The buddy does not assess performance and plays no part in
the introductory period review.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.4 CHECK-INS AND THE INTRODUCTORY REVIEW

Structured check-ins with your manager are held at 30, 60 and 90 days.

The 90-day check-in is also the formal introductory period review described in
document 2. Its outcome is one of three: confirmation in the role, an extension
of the introductory period by up to 30 days, or termination of employment.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.5 PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

Formal performance reviews are held twice a year, in April and October.

Each review covers delivery against goals and how the employee works with
others, assessed against the four company values in document 1.

Reviews use a four-point scale: Below expectations, Meeting expectations,
Exceeding expectations, and Outstanding.

Every employee writes a self-assessment before the review. Managers collect
feedback from at least two colleagues the employee has worked with.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.6 PROMOTION

Promotion means moving to a higher salary band, as described in document 3.

Promotions are decided in the April and October review cycles. A manager puts
forward a case, which is reviewed by the department Vice President and by
People Operations to check that the standard is applied consistently across
teams.

There is no minimum time in band before promotion.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.7 WHEN PERFORMANCE FALLS SHORT

Where performance is below expectations, the manager and employee agree a
performance improvement plan setting out what must change, what support will be
given, and how progress will be measured.

A performance improvement plan runs for 60 days. It is reviewed every two weeks
and ends in either a return to good standing or termination of employment.

A performance improvement plan is never the first conversation about a problem.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.8 LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

Each employee has a professional development budget of 1,500 US dollars per
fiscal year for books, courses, certifications and similar.

The budget does not carry over between fiscal years. Spend it through the
expense system with manager approval, following the rules in document 3.

Employees may additionally attend one professional conference per fiscal year.
Registration and travel for an approved conference come from the company travel
budget and not from the 1,500 US dollar development budget.

Internal technical talks are held every second Wednesday at 15:00 Eastern Time
and are open to everyone.
