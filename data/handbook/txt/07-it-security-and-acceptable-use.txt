LARKFIELD SYSTEMS EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Document 7 of 8: IT Security and Acceptable Use
Revision 4.2 - Effective 1 January 2026

NOTICE: Larkfield Systems is a fictional company. This handbook is synthetic
material written for testing the OnboardAI assistant. It contains no real
employee, company or customer data, and nothing in it is legal advice.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 SCOPE

This document applies to every device and account used for Larkfield Systems
work, whether the company owns it or you do.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.2 ACCOUNTS AND PASSWORDS

Multi-factor authentication is required on every company account. No exceptions
are granted.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. The company password manager is
provided to every employee and must be used for work credentials; storing work
passwords in a browser or a personal note is not permitted.

Passwords are not rotated on a schedule. They are changed only when there is
reason to believe one has been exposed.

Never share a password or an authentication code, including with IT. No member
of IT staff will ever ask you for one.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.3 DEVICES

Company laptops are encrypted at rest before they are issued, and the screen
lock is set to engage after 10 minutes of inactivity. Neither setting may be
turned off.

Laptops are replaced every three years, or sooner if the hardware fails.

Install software from the company application catalogue. Anything not in the
catalogue needs IT approval before installation.

Report a lost or stolen device to IT immediately, at any hour, so that it can
be wiped remotely.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.4 HOW WE CLASSIFY INFORMATION

Every piece of company information falls into one of four levels:

  Public         Approved for release outside the company. Marketing material,
                 published documentation.

  Internal       Shared freely inside the company, not outside it. Team plans,
                 internal announcements, this handbook.

  Confidential   Limited to those who need it. Customer data, contracts,
                 unreleased product plans, salary bands for individuals.

  Restricted     Limited to named individuals. Authentication secrets,
                 encryption keys, security incident detail, employee medical
                 and personnel records.

Confidential and Restricted information may not be copied to personal email,
personal cloud storage or personal devices.

When you are unsure of the level, treat the information as Confidential and
ask the owner.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.5 ACCEPTABLE USE

Limited personal use of company devices is permitted provided it does not
interfere with your work, consume significant resources, or involve anything
unlawful or offensive.

Company systems may not be used to run a personal business, to mine
cryptocurrency, or to store personal media libraries.

Larkfield Systems may access company systems and the data on them when there is
a legitimate business or legal reason. You should not expect privacy in
material stored on company systems.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.6 EXTERNAL SERVICES AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS

Using a new external service to process company information requires a security
review by the Security team before any data is sent to it. Allow 10 business
days.

Confidential and Restricted information may not be entered into any external
artificial intelligence tool that has not completed that review.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.7 REPORTING A SECURITY INCIDENT

Report a suspected security incident within one hour of noticing it, to
security@larkfieldsystems.example or through the IT service desk. A suspected
incident includes a lost device, a clicked phishing link, an unexpected
password prompt, or information sent to the wrong recipient.

Reporting quickly is always the right call. No employee has ever been
disciplined at Larkfield Systems for reporting an incident promptly and
honestly, including one they caused themselves.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.8 SECURITY TRAINING

All employees complete security awareness training within the first 14 days of
employment and once every year after that.

Simulated phishing exercises are run quarterly. Failing one results in a short
refresher module, not a disciplinary measure.
