6.1 WHAT WE EXPECT

Every employee is expected to act honestly, to treat colleagues and customers
with respect, and to raise concerns early. This code applies to employees,
contractors and anyone acting on behalf of Larkfield Systems.

Breaching this code may lead to disciplinary action up to and including
dismissal.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.2 A WORKPLACE FREE OF HARASSMENT

Larkfield Systems does not tolerate harassment or discrimination on the basis
of race, colour, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national
origin, age, disability, veteran status, marital status, pregnancy, or genetic
information.

Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that a reasonable person would find
intimidating, hostile or offensive. It applies equally in offices, on video
calls, in written messages and at company events.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.3 HOW TO REPORT A CONCERN

There are three routes, and you may use whichever you prefer:

  1. Your manager.
  2. People Operations, at people@larkfieldsystems.example.
  3. The anonymous ethics line, reachable by phone or web form 24 hours a day.
     Contact details are on the intranet. Reports may be made without giving
     your name.

People Operations acknowledges a report within two business days.
Investigations are completed within 30 calendar days where practicable; where
more time is needed the reporter is told why.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.4 NON-RETALIATION

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith, or who takes
part in an investigation, is itself a serious breach of this code and is
treated as a dismissal matter.

A report that turns out to be mistaken is not a breach. A report made in bad
faith, knowing it to be false, is.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.5 CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

A conflict of interest arises where your personal interests could influence, or
appear to influence, a decision you make for Larkfield Systems.

Disclose any actual or potential conflict to your manager and to the Legal team
within 10 business days of becoming aware of it. Common examples:

  - A financial interest in a customer, supplier or competitor.
  - A close personal relationship with someone whose work you assess or whose
    contract you influence.
  - Outside work that competes with the company.

Disclosure is not an accusation. Most disclosed conflicts are managed simply,
often by moving a decision to someone else.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.6 GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY

You may accept a gift or hospitality from a customer or supplier only where its
value is 100 US dollars or less and it could not reasonably be seen as
influencing a decision.

Anything above that value must be declined, or declared to the Legal team if
declining would cause offence. Cash and cash equivalents may never be accepted,
at any value.

Nothing may be offered or accepted during an active procurement or contract
negotiation.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.7 OUTSIDE EMPLOYMENT

Employees may hold outside employment provided it does not compete with
Larkfield Systems, does not use company time or equipment, and does not affect
their work here.

Outside employment must be approved in writing by your manager before it
begins. Serving on the board of another company, paid or unpaid, requires
approval from the Legal team.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.8 COMPANY PROPERTY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

Company information stays confidential both during and after employment.
Document 7 sets out how information must be classified and handled.

Speaking publicly on behalf of Larkfield Systems, including to the press,
requires prior approval. Personal social media use is your own business
provided it is clear you speak for yourself.
