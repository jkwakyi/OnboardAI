3.1 PAY SCHEDULE

Employees are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th of the month and on the last
business day of the month. Where the 15th falls on a weekend or a company
holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day.

Payment is by direct deposit. Bank details are changed through the payroll
portal; a change made fewer than five business days before a pay date takes
effect from the following pay date.

Payslips are available in the payroll portal on the morning of each pay date
and are retained there for seven years.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 SALARY STRUCTURE

Every role is mapped to one of six salary bands, numbered L1 to L6. Band
ranges are reviewed each November against market data and published to all
employees on the intranet in December.

Your band is stated in your offer letter and confirmed at every performance
review. Moving band is a promotion and is described in document 8.

Salary increases take effect on 1 February, the first day of the fiscal year.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 OVERTIME

Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5 times their regular hourly rate for hours
worked beyond 40 in a work week.

Overtime must be approved in writing by your manager before it is worked.
Unapproved overtime is still paid, in line with the law, but is treated as a
performance matter.

Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 THE REFERRAL BONUS

Employees who refer a candidate that Larkfield Systems hires receive a referral
bonus of 2,000 US dollars.

The bonus is paid on the pay date following the referred employee completing
90 days of employment. Both the referrer and the referred employee must still
be employed on that date.

Members of the Revenue department talent team and anyone involved in the hiring
decision for that role are not eligible.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 BUSINESS EXPENSES

Reasonable business expenses are reimbursed. Submit claims in the expense
system with an itemised receipt.

  - Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
    Claims submitted later require Vice President approval.
  - Any single expense over 500 US dollars requires manager approval before it
    is incurred.
  - Reimbursement is paid with the next semi-monthly payroll run after
    approval.

The daily meal allowance when travelling for business is 70 US dollars.
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Mileage in a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at the standard
federal rate published each January.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 PAYROLL ERRORS

Report a suspected error to payroll@larkfieldsystems.example within 10 business
days of the pay date. Confirmed underpayments are corrected by an off-cycle
payment within three business days of confirmation.
