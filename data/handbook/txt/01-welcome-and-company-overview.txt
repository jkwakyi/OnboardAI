1.2 WHO WE ARE

Larkfield Systems was founded in 2014 and builds inventory forecasting software
for mid-sized manufacturers. We employ 240 people.

Our headquarters is at 1100 Fenway Street, Baltimore, Maryland. We maintain a
second office in Portland, Oregon, which opened in 2019. All other employees
work remotely within the United States.

The Chief Executive Officer is Dana Whitfield, who co-founded the company. The
Chief Technology Officer is Marcus Oyelaran. The Head of People Operations is
Priya Raghunathan.

1.3 HOW WE ARE ORGANISED

The company has five departments:

  - Engineering
  - Product and Design
  - Customer Success
  - Revenue
  - Corporate Operations, which contains People Operations, Finance and Legal

Each department is led by a Vice President who reports to the Chief Executive
Officer. Teams within a department are normally six to ten people.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 OUR VALUES

Four values guide how we work:

  Evidence over instinct.   We look at the data before we decide.
  Say the hard thing early. We raise problems while they are still small.
  Leave it better.          We improve what we touch, including documentation.
  Respect the whole person. Work is part of a life, not the whole of one.

Values are discussed at every performance review. See document 8.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.5 THE COMPANY CALENDAR

Our fiscal year runs from 1 February to 31 January.

A company-wide all-hands meeting is held on the first Thursday of every month
at 11:00 Eastern Time. It is recorded and posted to the intranet within one
business day for employees who cannot attend live.

Two in-person company gatherings are held each year, one in Baltimore in May
and one in Portland in November. Travel is paid by the company. Attendance is
encouraged but not required.
