Document 5 of 8: Benefits and Wellbeing

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 ELIGIBILITY AND ENROLMENT

Regular full-time employees are eligible for the benefits in this document from
their first day of employment. There is no waiting period.

You have 30 days from your start date to enrol. An employee who does not enrol
within 30 days must wait for the next open enrolment period, which runs for two
weeks each November for cover beginning 1 January.

Outside open enrolment, cover may be changed within 30 days of a qualifying
life event such as marriage, the birth or adoption of a child, or the loss of
other cover.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 MEDICAL AND DENTAL COVER

Two medical plans are offered, a Standard plan and a High Deductible plan with
a health savings account.

Larkfield Systems pays 80 per cent of the premium for employee-only cover and
50 per cent of the premium for dependent cover, on either plan.

Dental cover is offered as a single plan. The company pays 80 per cent of the
employee-only premium.

Plan documents, including deductibles and co-payment amounts, are published on
the benefits portal. People Operations cannot advise on which plan to choose.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 RETIREMENT SAVINGS

Larkfield Systems offers a 401(k) plan. The company matches employee
contributions up to 4 per cent of base salary.

The match vests immediately. There is no vesting schedule and no service
requirement.

Employees may begin, change or stop contributions at any time through the
retirement provider portal. Changes take effect from the next pay date.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 WELLNESS STIPEND

A wellness stipend of 75 US dollars per month is available to all regular
full-time employees.

It may be used for gym membership, fitness classes, exercise equipment,
meditation or therapy applications, or similar. Claim it through the expense
system with a receipt.

The stipend does not accumulate; an unclaimed month is lost.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.5 THE EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME

The Employee Assistance Programme provides six free counselling sessions per
employee per calendar year, for the employee and members of their household.

The programme also offers legal and financial consultations. It is operated by
an external provider and is confidential: Larkfield Systems receives only
aggregate usage numbers and never the identity of anyone who uses it.

The programme is available 24 hours a day. Contact details are on the benefits
portal.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.6 EQUIPMENT AND WORKSPACE

Every employee receives a laptop and the peripherals their role requires.
Document 7 covers how that equipment must be secured.

Remote and hybrid employees receive a home office stipend of 500 US dollars in
their first year, and 250 US dollars in each following year, for desk, chair,
lighting and similar items.

A mobile phone stipend of 40 US dollars per month is paid to employees whose
role requires them to be reachable by phone outside core hours. Eligibility is
confirmed by your manager.

Items bought with the home office stipend remain the property of Larkfield
Systems and must be returned if their value exceeded 250 US dollars.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.7 WHEN BENEFITS END

Cover ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. Information
about continuing cover at your own expense is sent by the benefits
administrator within 14 days of your last working day.
